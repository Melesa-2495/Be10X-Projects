## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a clinical yet appetizing aesthetic for a health-conscious, safety-first food delivery application. The design style merges **Minimalism** with a **Corporate / Modern** framework, emphasizing clarity, high legibility, and trustworthy medical-grade precision alongside warm, high-fidelity food imagery.

The personality is authoritative, transparent, and nurturing. It evokes a feeling of absolute safety, confidence, and effortless wellness. The interface utilizes high-contrast typography, surgical whitespace, and purposeful, data-driven color coding to ensure nutritional metrics and allergen disclosures are communicated instantly without cognitive overload.

## Colors

The palette balances clinical precision with natural vitality. 

- **Primary (`#0F52BA` - Deep Sapphire):** Conveys absolute medical-grade authority, security, and systemic reliability for safety data.
- **Secondary (`#2E8B57` - Forest Green):** Represents health, verified nutrition, and organic freshness.
- **Tertiary (`#E06D2D` - Warm Amber):** Deployed specifically for caution states, estimated values, and dietary warnings.
- **Neutral (`#F4F6F8` - Crisp Clinical White / Ice Gray):** Creates high-contrast, glare-free backdrops that make food photography pop while maintaining a sterile, clean laboratory feel.

### Data Confidence Tiers
- **Verified:** Forest Green (`#2E8B57`) backgrounds and text indicators.
- **Restaurant-Declared:** Deep Sapphire (`#0F52BA`) indicators.
- **Estimated:** Warm Amber (`#E06D2D`) mixed with neutral gray states.

## Typography

The typography system relies exclusively on **Inter** for its neutral, highly legible, and utilitarian character. Designed for rapid scannability, the scale features oversized numeric weights (`numeric-hero`) explicitly engineered for tracking macros, calorie counts, and confidence scores at a glance. 

Maintain strict adherence to the defined line heights to ensure optical balance between dense nutritional data and inviting culinary descriptions.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a **Fluid grid** model with a strict 8px baseline rhythm. 

- **Breakpoints:** Mobile (< 640px), Tablet (640px - 1024px), Desktop (> 1024px).
- **Gutters & Margins:** On mobile, outer canvas margins lock to `1.5rem` with `1rem` gutters. Tablet and desktop layouts expand outer margins to `2.5rem` while preserving a max-content width to prevent eye tracking fatigue when reading complex ingredient matrices.
- **Component Spacing:** Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for internal micro-padding (badges, tags), and `space-md` through `space-xl` for sectional separation on safety disclosures and menu cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated primarily through **Low-contrast outlines** combined with **Tonal layers**. Shadows are used sparingly to avoid visual clutter. 

- **Surfaces:** Utilize pure white cards (`#FFFFFF`) layered directly over the light neutral background (`#F4F6F8`).
- **Borders:** Define structural containers using soft, low-opacity ghost borders (`1px solid rgba(15, 82, 186, 0.12)`) to mimic clinical data sheets.
- **Elevated States:** Modals and sticky checkout bars employ diffused, low-opacity ambient shadows tinted with the primary sapphire tone to anchor interactive elements above the document flow.

## Shapes

A soft shape language (`roundedness`: 1) balances medical precision with human approachability. 

- **UI Elements:** Interactive components, cards, and input fields feature a gentle `0.25rem` radius, scaling up to `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) for container cards and `0.75rem` (`rounded-xl`) for bottom sheets and floating action modules.
- **Confidence Badges:** Dietary badges and macro tags utilize pill shapes to clearly isolate verified data points from structural UI elements.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Sapphire (`#0F52BA`) background with white text, `0.25rem` rounded corners, and a clear hover state shift. Used for critical conversion actions (e.g., "Add to Bag", "Proceed to Checkout").
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Transparent background with a 1px primary border and sapphire text. Used for secondary actions like "View Full Lab Breakdown".
- **Warning / Alert:** Warm Amber variants reserved explicitly for allergy confirmation dialogs.

### Chips & Badges
- Color-coded explicitly for data confidence:
  - **Verified (Green):** Forest green background tint with solid green text and an accompanying checkmark icon.
  - **Restaurant-Declared (Blue):** Sapphire tint with solid blue text.
  - **Estimated (Amber):** Amber tint with solid amber text and an info icon.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- High-contrast square checkboxes (`0.25rem` radius) for multi-select allergen filters (e.g., *Gluten-Free*, *Nut-Free*). Selected states fill entirely in Deep Sapphire with a crisp white checkmark.

### Input Fields
- Clinical input styling: light neutral background, 1px subtle border, and a clear floating label. Error states shift the border instantly to Warm Amber with an adjacent helper text explaining safety implications.

### Cards
- **Restaurant Card:** White surface, subtle ghost border, featuring large photography, safety score prominently displayed via a numeric badge, and clear delivery time metrics.
- **Dish Card:** Compact horizontal layout emphasizing macro-nutrients (`numeric-hero` style sizing for calories) and an unmissable allergen warning strip at the base.

### Additional Components
- **Allergen Matrix Drawer:** A bottom sheet component designed to display cross-contamination risks and ingredient source traceability in an easy-to-read tabular format.
